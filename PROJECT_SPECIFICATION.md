## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

Traditional paper wedding cards aur static image/PDF/video invitations outdated ho chuke hain:
- Paper cards me lakho rupaye kharch hote hain, environment ko nuksan hota hai, aur map/directions nahi hoti.
- Static WhatsApp images/PDFs me audio nahi chalta, venue ka navigation click nahi hota, aur RSVP track nahi hota.

**Project INVITE** ek Next-Generation **Interactive 3D Micro-Website Platform** hai jahan koi bhi host ya couple 2 minute ke andar apna personalized, luxury digital invitation create kar sakta hai aur WhatsApp par ek link ke zariye hazaron guests ko bhej sakta hai.

## 3. 🔥 Hamare 10 Killer EXTRA Features (The USPs)

Market ke baaki players sirf ek sundar webpage banakar chhod dete hain. Hum is platform ko ek **Complete Wedding/Event Companion** bana rahe hain:

### 1. 💰 Digital Shagun / Gift Registry via Direct UPI
* **Problem:** Jo relatives ya friends door rehte hain ya shaadi attend nahi kar paate, wo shagun bhejne ke liye account details maangte hain.
* **Our Solution:** Webpage par ek elegant *"Send Shagun / Blessings"* section. Guest amount (₹501, ₹1100, ₹2100, ₹5100) select karta hai, ek **Animated 3D Virtual Gold Envelope** khulta hai aur direct couple ke UPI QR code (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm) se instant transfer ho jata hai (**0% commission to us, 100% direct to couple**).

### 2. 📸 Crowdsourced Live Wedding Photo Stream
* **Problem:** Shaadi me aane wale guests dher saari candid photos aur selfies lete hain jo couple ko kabhi nahi milti. Official photographer ki photos aane me 2 mahine lagte hain.
* **Our Solution:** Webpage par **"Live Memories Stream"**. Guests bina kisi app download ke apne phone se wedding photos/videos direct upload kar sakte hain. Host ke paas 1-click "Download All High-Res" ka option hota hai.

### 3. 📲 1-Click Personalized WhatsApp Dispatcher
* **Problem:** 500 guests ko alag-alag message copy-paste karke bhejna sabse bada sir-dard hai.
* **Our Solution:** Host ek Excel/CSV upload karega (e.g. "Ramesh Sharma Ji", "Rahul Uncle"). System har guest ke naam ka personalized link aur message generate karke 1-click me direct unke WhatsApp par bhej dega: *"Namaste Sharma Ji, Kabir & Ananya aapko parivaar sahit nimantran dete hain..."*.

### 4. 🤖 AI Love Story & Bio Generator (Multilingual)
* **Problem:** Couples ko samajh nahi aata ki unki love story ya invitation bio kaise poetic aur touching likhein.
* **Our Solution:** Creator form me sirf 3 sawal: *"Aap kahan mile? First date kahan thi? Shaadi ka vibe kya hai?"*. AI instant 3 variations generate karega: **Royal Traditional**, **Poetic Romantic**, aur **Fun & Modern** (Hindi, English, aur Hinglish me).

### 5. 🚗 Smart Maps with 1-Tap Uber / Ola Integration
* Standard Google Maps ke alawa, page par ek **"Book Cab to Venue"** button hoga jo user ke phone me direct Uber/Ola app khol kar destination auto-fill kar dega.

### 6. 🎟️ Digital QR Guest Pass (For Destination & Premium Weddings)
* Destination weddings ya banquet security ke liye, RSVP confirm hote hi guest ko digital pass milega jisse gate par entry coordinator scan karke room number aur welcome kit verify kar sake.

### 7. 🌐 Multi-Language Bilingual Switcher
* Indian weddings me bade-buzurg Hindi ya regional script prefer karte hain aur friends English. Top-right me ek clean toggle: **[ English | हिन्दी | ગુજરાતી | ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ]**. 1-click par poora text switch ho jata hai.

### 8. ⏰ "Add to Calendar" with Auto WhatsApp Reminder
* Guest **"Add to Google Calendar / iCal"** par click karega toh event calendar me sync ho jayega. Event se 1 din pehle automated gentle WhatsApp reminder chala jayega.

### 9. 🎵 Custom Song Upload & Ambient Audio Player
* Couple apna manpasand gaana (e.g., *Din Shagna Da*, *Kudmayi*, ya retro flute) MP3 upload kar sakte hain ya curated Indian wedding instrumental library se select kar sakte hain.

### 10. 📊 Host Event Intelligence Dashboard
* Host ke phone par real-time analytics:
  * Kitne logo ne link open kiya?
  * Total attending vs not attending headcount (Catering headcount planning ke liye).
  * Guests ke wishes aur messages ka beautiful printable PDF album.

## 6. Recommended Technical Architecture

* **Frontend:** Next.js 14/15 (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons.
* **3D Engine & Canvas:** Three.js (WebGL 3D Palace Gates, metallic lighting, camera zoom), HTML5 Canvas (Gold Foil Scratch Card).
* **Animations:** Framer Motion (page transitions, 3D tilt cards, shagun envelope modal).
* **Database & Realtime:** Supabase (PostgreSQL) for storing event details, RSVPs, and live photo URLs.
* **Payments:** Razorpay (UPI, QR, Cards, NetBanking).
* **Hosting & Edge Delivery:** Vercel + Cloudflare CDN (Sub-1.5 second loading speed globally).
